# Wishlist visitor processing and legal-basis proposal

Status: **draft for product/privacy and legal review**, 2026-10-02. This is a decision proposal, not an approved legal position or a published privacy notice. Tracks [WNT-85](https://linear.app/wastingnotime/issue/WNT-85/wishlist-close-privacy-requirements-before-public-voting) and [WNT-86](https://linear.app/wastingnotime/issue/WNT-86/wishlist-document-processing-basis-and-show-a-privacy-notice).

## Scope and facts observed

Wishlist lets anyone browse the public board. Voting and private suggestion submission require a verified email. The browser sends the email and OTP through a same-origin BFF to the Go API. The API stores visitor records in PostgreSQL and sends the OTP through the production SMTP adapter. The admin API exposes pending suggestion text to authorized WNT reviewers. Accepting a suggestion creates a public feature from reviewed text; merging associates the private suggestion with an existing feature. Public feature responses contain aggregate counts, not voter identities. Votes inform WNT judgment and never change feature lifecycle automatically.

Evidence: [`001_initial.sql`](../../../apps/api/internal/infrastructure/migrations/001_initial.sql), [`visitor.go`](../../../apps/api/internal/application/visitor.go), [`postgres_store.go`](../../../apps/api/internal/infrastructure/postgres_store.go), [`server.go`](../../../apps/api/internal/httpapi/server.go), [`index.tsx`](../../../apps/web/src/routes/index.tsx), and the [product handoff](../../sources/wnt-wishlist-handoff.md).

## Proposed LGPD treatment inventory

Each basis below is a **candidate**, not a declaration that the legal test has been met. The product/privacy owner and legal reviewer must approve the basis and its necessity assessment before public voting. A legitimate-interest choice needs a documented purpose, necessity, expected impact on visitors, safeguards, and balancing result. OTP verification is proof of email control; it is not consent to marketing or unrelated processing.

| Activity and purpose | Data and people | Proposed LGPD basis | Recipients and safeguards to validate | Retention proposal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Request and verify OTP so a person can vote or suggest once per identity | Visitor email, challenge ID, keyed code digest, expiry, failed attempts, request timestamps; SMTP provider receives recipient and message with code | Art. 7 IX, legitimate interest in access control and abuse prevention, **subject to balancing**. Confirm whether a different basis better describes this requested service. | BFF, API, PostgreSQL, SMTP provider. Code must not enter production logs. | See [retention proposal](retention-and-deletion.md): short-lived challenge and throttle history. |
| Maintain signed-in visitor access | Identity ID, normalized email, opaque session token, expiry; browser cookie | Art. 7 IX, legitimate interest in maintaining verified access, **subject to balancing**. | BFF/API/PostgreSQL; HttpOnly, Secure production cookie; limited database access. | Session validity is 30 days; expired-row deletion is proposed separately. |
| Enforce one vote and display a demand signal | Identity-to-feature vote link and timestamp; public aggregate count | Art. 7 IX, legitimate interest in measuring feature demand and preventing duplicate votes, **subject to balancing**. | WNT reviewers see the demand signal; the public sees aggregate counts only. A voter identity must not appear in public API responses. | Linked vote retention and erasure semantics require approval; see retention proposal. |
| Receive and review a private suggestion | Identity link, app, title, description, status, creation/review dates, resulting feature link | Art. 7 IX, legitimate interest in reviewing requested ideas, **subject to balancing**. | Authorized WNT reviewers; avoid unnecessary personal data in free text. | Pending and reviewed suggestion deadlines require approval; see retention proposal. |
| Publish a reviewed idea as a public feature | Reviewed title and description, feature metadata; may contain personal information supplied in free text | **Open choice.** Decide whether publication can rely on a documented legitimate-interest assessment with prepublication redaction and clear notice, or needs an additional specific user choice. | Public board. Reviewers must remove personal or third-party details before publication. | Public feature content has its own product retention; private source link follows suggestion policy. |
| Detect abuse, diagnose failures, and investigate incidents | Potential IP, request metadata, error records, provider logs; exact fields are not yet inventoried | Art. 7 IX for proportionate security operations, **subject to balancing**; legal obligation only for a specific mandatory record or incident process once identified. | API/BFF/reverse proxy/hosting/SMTP logs and authorized operators. No OTP, session token, or unnecessary email in production logs. | Operational log duration and locations require infra review. |
| Respond to verified privacy requests | Requester contact, verification evidence, request, action and exception record | Art. 7 II, compliance with a legal obligation, **subject to legal confirmation of the specific obligation and minimum record**. | Authorized privacy operator and processors needed to fulfill the request. | A minimal case-record period and exception handling require approval. |

No marketing, analytics, or optional tracking purpose is established by this inventory. Adding one requires a separate purpose, basis, and notice review. A stored identity ID or stable hash remains personal data if WNT can link it back to a voter.

## Jurisdiction and notice decisions

The first release should meet the LGPD baseline. **User direction, 2026-10-02: defer GDPR implementation and make Wishlist publicly available without regional access limits.** This sets implementation priority and product reach; it is not a finding that GDPR cannot apply. Record the initial operating jurisdictions: where the controller is established and processing occurs, where the product is intentionally offered, where visitor data is collected, and where relevant vendors process it. Public worldwide access does not alone establish EU targeting, but the absence of a region limit means people in the EU can use the email, voting, and suggestion flows. Before public launch, obtain a short legal review of whether the actual offer or any behavioral monitoring brings those flows within GDPR Article 3. If it does, GDPR requirements cannot be postponed for affected processing merely by labeling the project LGPD-first; adjust launch scope or implement the applicable requirements. If it does not, document the evidence and reassess when audience, language, promotion, analytics, or product behavior changes.

### Small-agent exemption assessment

**Provisional conclusion: Wastingnotime appears eligible for the small-agent exemption from formally appointing an *encarregado*, based on the facts supplied and the preliminary Wishlist risk assessment below.** The owner supplied Receita Federal details showing ME classification and reports current annual revenue projected below R$180,000; this is a projection, not a completed-year accounting figure, and actual gross revenue should be checked as the company grows. The owner confirmed Wastingnotime is a solo company with no other company in its economic group; record that as an owner attestation, not as a conclusion drawn from CNAE codes. Simples Nacional enrollment is corroborating context, not a substitute for the ANPD's exclusions. Preserve evidence sufficient to demonstrate eligibility if ANPD requests it, and reassess the risk finding when the product or scale changes. Even with the exemption, the company must keep a functioning rights-request channel and meet the other LGPD obligations.

The owner also supplied CNAEs 6204-0/00 (IT consulting) and 6201-5/01 (custom software development). These describe the company's activities, **not** economic-group membership. Multiple software products or repositories operated within the same company are not separate companies merely because they are different products. The owner confirms there is no other company in an economic group with Wastingnotime. Recheck if ownership or corporate structure changes.

**Preliminary Wishlist high-risk assessment, 2026-10-02:** the currently described processing does **not appear to meet** ANPD Resolution 2/2022 art. 4's high-risk test. This is a product assessment for review, not a final legal classification. The regulation requires at least one general criterion **and** one specific criterion.

| Criterion | Current assessment and evidence to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| General: large-scale processing | Not established. The production readiness record says release is not yet eligible; projected visitor count, record volume, frequency, duration, and geographic spread after launch are unknown. Worldwide availability alone does not prove large scale. |
| General: significant effect on rights/interests | Not apparent from idea votes and private suggestions. Email verification and automated OTP throttling can temporarily prevent participation, but no evidence shows effects of the kind or severity described by art. 4 §2. Reassess if Wishlist becomes consequential for access to services or people. |
| Specific: emerging or innovative technology | No such processing identified in the current Go API, PostgreSQL, BFF, and email OTP flow. Reassess if new analytics, profiling, or other technology is added. |
| Specific: surveillance/control of public spaces | None identified. A public website is not surveillance of a physical public space. |
| Specific: decisions solely from automated personal-data processing | Vote totals rank **features**, while human admins decide their lifecycle and moderate suggestions. OTP throttling is automated and should be considered in the complete assessment, but it is not currently shown to have a significant personal effect. |
| Specific: sensitive data or data of children, adolescents, or older people | Not an intended data category, but the free-text suggestion field can receive such data and there is no verified age gate. Document the intended audience, discourage unnecessary personal/sensitive details, and define review/redaction/deletion of submitted text. Reassess if these data are actually used or the audience changes. |

Revisit this assessment before public voting and whenever scale, audience, suggestion handling, or automated decisions change. A single sensitive submission or a broad geographic reach does not by itself satisfy both parts of the regulatory test, but it still needs appropriate handling. [ANPD Resolution 2/2022, art. 4](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/institucional/atos-normativos/regulamentacoes_anpd/resolucao-cd-anpd-no-2-de-27-de-janeiro-de-2022).

The present email form says only that the address stays private. It does not explain the purposes, basis, recipients, retention, rights channel, or possible publication of reviewed suggestion text. After approval, [WNT-86](https://linear.app/wastingnotime/issue/WNT-86/wishlist-document-processing-basis-and-show-a-privacy-notice) should put a concise notice beside the email field and link to a full product notice. The organization policy and contact from [WNT-90](https://linear.app/wastingnotime/issue/WNT-90/wnt-establish-organization-wide-privacy-policy-and-rights-channel) and pattern from [WNT-91](https://linear.app/wastingnotime/issue/WNT-91/wnt-define-product-specific-privacy-notice-and-consent-pattern) remain dependencies. Do not add a generic consent checkbox as a substitute for notice.
