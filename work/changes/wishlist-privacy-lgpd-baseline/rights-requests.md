# Wishlist visitor rights requests (WNT-88)

Status: **application workflow implemented locally; production release, legal review, and external-copy reconciliation pending**, 2026-10-03. This record defines product behavior; it is not an approved legal position or a published privacy notice. The requester can use `/privacy` or contact `privacy@wastingnotime.org`. GDPR applicability work is deferred by owner direction; the public service is not region-limited, so that question remains a release decision.

## Identity verification and response

- A visitor requests an OTP at their existing address. Successful verification creates a new session. Access, email correction, and erasure require a session created by OTP verification within the last 10 minutes; the ordinary 30-day session is insufficient after that window. OTP expires after 10 minutes and allows five failed attempts. A privacy response is never returned through a public API response or logged as an email-bearing event.
- The `/privacy` page displays the linked email, account dates, active session count, votes, and pending/reviewed private suggestions. It lets the visitor change email after a separate OTP to the new address. An address already held by another identity cannot be substituted automatically; the owner handles that exception privately. The correction keeps the current session and signs out other sessions.
- An operator may handle requests received by email when self-service is unavailable. Before opening data, the operator verifies requester control of the recorded email or documents another appropriate identity check in a **private rights case**. The operator records a case reference, request, verification method and time, decision, action and response, plus any exception reason and deadline. Only an authorized Wishlist admin can use the `/admin` privacy request panel or `POST /v1/admin/privacy/data`, `/v1/admin/privacy/correct-email`, and `/v1/admin/privacy/erase`; all require the case reference. Before operator correction, verify the new address as well. Operator correction revokes every session and does not extend the last OTP verification date. The case reference is an input gate, **not a stored audit record**. The owner must maintain the case outside Wishlist in a restricted store. Do not paste personal details into Linear, public issues, or application logs.
- The operator checks whether a lawful exception applies before erasure. Any retained record needs its purpose, fields, access restriction, owner, and expiry in the private case, with privacy/legal review. The current application does not implement an exception hold; a case requiring one must be handled separately before using the erase action. Record the outcome and respond through the private channel. The proposed 12-month post-closure case retention remains subject to legal approval.

## Erasure semantics

Successful self-service or operator erasure removes the live identity and email, all sessions, OTP challenge and request history for that address, votes, and pending/reviewed private suggestion source records in one PostgreSQL transaction. Public vote totals decrease because votes are counted from rows; feature lifecycle never changes automatically. A later OTP verification creates a fresh identity and does not restore earlier votes or suggestions. No persistent identity hash or tombstone is kept in the Wishlist live store.

A published feature that came from an erased suggestion stays visible until a human checks its title and description for identifying details. Erasure queues that feature in `/admin` for review without carrying over the deleted identity or suggestion text. The operator edits or removes personal detail and marks the review complete. This public-text review, plus external copies, can remain open after the live-store transaction; the response must describe the actual scope of completion.

The live-store operation does not remove Mailgun records, proxy/application logs, legacy SQLite files, exports, or backup copies. Their locations, retention, and restore reconciliation are tracked by [WNT-89](https://linear.app/wastingnotime/issue/WNT-89/wishlist-review-processor-logging-and-backup-privacy-controls) and [WNT-102](https://linear.app/wastingnotime/issue/WNT-102/wishlist-establish-and-test-postgresql-backup-and-restore-before). Before production use, the privacy owner and runtime owner must agree how the private case records approved erasures for restore reconciliation without silently reintroducing deleted data.
